October 23, 2026 — Manual Lineup Save Correction
The prior team editor disabled Save for positions outside player profiles, and the default-match optimizer could reshuffle saved choices. Both behaviors conflicted with the coach's requested manual control and are superseded by this correction.

- Team templates accept any roster player in any valid slot; duplicate players, invalid slots, and missing roster references still fail validation.
- Save Lineup directly saves custom positions in team, match setup, and ready-match editors without a position-approval popup. Match editors still require at least one starter; team templates may be saved empty. Storage failure remains visible and preserves the draft.
- New team templates open empty. Auto Pick is optional; it fills an empty field in one tap and asks before replacing an existing draft.
- Match setup treats available saved starters as fixed assignments, including custom roles. Only vacant positions receive eligible automatic replacements. Attendance changes preserve custom manual assignments, and missing players do not cause other starters to move.
- Ready-match field swaps save directly regardless of profile roles. Live-match substitution and timing confirmations remain in place. Player profiles are never silently changed by a lineup choice.
