Privacy Policy

Updated October 23, 2026

Your Coaching Data

PitchHarbor stores team and player details, approved positions, availability, match events, playing time, scores, boards, and setup drafts in the app’s private storage on your device. These details power your roster, match tracking, Smart Subs, and playbook.

The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated server. Coaching data is processed on your device and is not automatically sent to the developer. We do not sell coaching data.

Sharing and Backups

When you export a backup or board or share a report, the content goes to the destination you select through Apple’s share or file interfaces. Editable backups contain player names and all saved coaching data and are not password protected. Reports omit names, but jersey numbers may identify players to someone who knows the team. Share only with authorized recipients.

Choosing Save Image can request permission to add the board image to Photos; the app does not read your photo library. Your Apple device backup settings may include the app’s data. Copies saved to Files, cloud storage, other apps, or other people are governed by those services and remain outside PitchHarbor’s control.

Retention and Control

Data remains until you remove it, restore a different backup, or delete the app. Open Data & Settings → Erase All Local Data to remove all teams, matches, boards, and drafts from this installation. Finish any active match first. This does not delete exported files or device backups; manage those copies separately with their provider.

Local files use iOS Data Protection. Protect your device with a passcode. Enter only player information you need and have permission to use; nicknames work. PitchHarbor is a tool for coaches and does not create player accounts.

These Information Pages

The support and privacy pages are hosted using Netlify. The hosting service may process technical request information to deliver and secure these pages. I have not added analytics, advertising, or data-entry forms to these pages. Website requests are separate from the coaching records stored inside the app.

Support and Contact

PitchHarbor is developed and supported independently by {{developerName}}. Contact {{supportEmail}} for support or privacy questions. Information you voluntarily include in support correspondence is used to respond to your request. Support email is handled through Gmail. You can contact me to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention obligations. Avoid sending player records or backups unless necessary and authorized.

This policy describes version 1.0. If data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant App Store disclosures.
