Getting Started

Create a team, choose match defaults, and add a roster. You can skip players and add them later in Teams. Select each player’s preferred and alternative positions to improve Smart Subs recommendations.

Match Day

Set up a match, confirm attendance and starters, check your competition rules, then start at kickoff. Use the toolbar for goals, substitutions, pause, and End Half. Review each recommendation before confirming who is on the field. The referee controls official time. Smart Subs does not assess health, injury, or medical fitness.

An unstarted match can be discarded from Match Options. Match History and Return to Active Match are also in that menu. After an interrupted running clock, review the recovery estimate before continuing.

Practice and Teaching

Create and save editable tactics boards in Practice. Show Players expands the field; Hide Tools makes more room and Tools restores the toolbar. Divided Duo layouts keep the field and controls in separate usable regions. Use Swap Sides or Swap Panels for reach.

Backups and Moving Devices

Data & Settings → Export Editable Backup preserves your teams, matches, and editable boards. Transfer the file to the new device and choose Restore Backup. Restore replaces the current local archive after confirmation, so export it first if needed. A board image is a picture and cannot restore your playbook.

If saving fails, keep the app open, free device storage, and tap Retry. If saved data cannot be opened, the app preserves it and offers restore. Do not delete the app before securing a backup if you need its data.

Data Control

Erase All Local Data in Settings removes this installation’s coaching data and restarts setup. Finish any active match first. Exports and device backups must be deleted separately. No account is needed.
