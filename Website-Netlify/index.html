02 / Smart Subs

Know Who’s Next.
Keep the Choice Yours.

A short bench makes every change matter. Get suggestions based on the players you have, the positions they cover, and their time on and off the field.

  1. 1

    Set Their Positions

    Give players a preferred position and the other roles they can cover.

  2. 2

    Choose Your Rotation Targets

    Set playing-stint and minimum bench-rest targets for your team.

  3. 3

    Review. Then Confirm.

    See who comes off, who goes on, and the position they’ll play. Confirm when the change happens.

Smart Subs follows your rotation preferences. It does not measure fatigue, assess injuries, or determine physical recovery.
